Delta near the barrier -- the hedging nightmare

As spot approaches the barrier the UAO delta collapses through zero and turns negative (price peaks near S/H = 0.90 and falls to zero at the barrier), while the vanilla delta climbs to 0.92. A desk long this option is short gamma and short vega in a long call -- hedging it with the vanilla playbook loses money in both directions.

Structure

3y NIFTY autocallable reverse convertible, quarterly observations. Autocall at 100% of initial fixing; coupon (non-memory) paid iff fixing >= 70%; capital at risk iff final fixing < 60% (European knock-in), redemption then scales with the index. Decomposition at par: redemption leg 94.97 + coupon annuity 70.7 x coupon = 100.

Model risk, stated

Barriers monitored at grid dates only are systematically overpriced; the engine applies the Brownian-bridge (Broadie-Glasserman-Kou) correction and the PDE -- which has no monitoring grid -- is the referee. Every price ships with a Monte Carlo standard error; Greeks use common random numbers so bump differences are signal, not noise.

Research code for education and portfolio demonstration. Not investment advice; structured products embed issuer credit risk and fees that this model deliberately isolates rather than hides.
